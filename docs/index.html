Personal YouTube upload tool

This is a personal automation tool used by Hitansh Gopani to upload prepared videos to his own Alpha Explorer YouTube channel. It is not a service for other users and does not offer public account registration.

The Google authorization requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. This lets the tool upload videos and their titles, descriptions, tags and visibility settings to the YouTube account selected by the owner during consent.

The upload authorization does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or comment-management permissions. Video preparation is separate from this authorization.
